Undertale's Story

In Undertale, the story is split between 2 sections, and one of those sections split into three more. The first two sections are the Past and Present, with the Present being able to split into three different timelines, usually called "Routes" and that is what I will being refering them as. Another part of the story is the music, showing both already known and unknown information, with lietmotifs.

The Past

This part of the story starts in the Surface, the original home of both Humans and Monsters. It was peace. Then a war broke out between the races. At the end of it, Humans won. They sealed the Monsters into the Underground with magic, needing 7 Human SOULs to break it, under a mountain called Mt. Ebbot, which is the main setting of Undertale. After being sealed, monster set up a civilization, with Asgore Dreamurr as King and Toriel Dreamurr as Queen, in New Home, which originally was in the Ruins. Asriel Dreamurr was born in the Underground, and there was peace.

Then a human fell down. This human name was Chara. Injured, Chara called for help. Asriel found Chara, and brung them home. The King and Queen welcomed this human child, beliving in kindness and compassion. Not only did Asriel and Chara become siblings, but best freinds. One day, Chara fell terminally ill. As a final wish, Chara wanted to see the golden flowers in thier village. After thier death, Asriel absorbed Chara's SOUL. Asriel carried Chara's body across the Barrier, the reason monsterkind cannot go to the Surface, and was attacked by Humans. Asriel fled, refusing to fight back. Upon his return, Asriel died from his injuries. When a monster dies it turns to dust, as Asriel did.

In a fit of rage, after losing 2 children in the same day, Asgore declared war upon Humankind. Toriel, did not agree with this choice. All Monsterkind in New Home left except for Toriel, turning into the Ruins. They moved to the location of The Cidatel. Then overtime, more human children fell into the Underground, and after escaping Toriel's care out of the Ruins, got to The Cidadel, and killed for the purpose of breaking the barrier with thier SOULs. The 2nd Royal Scientist, Alphys, started experiments upon Chara's SOUL, which was called Determination. After what was considered a failed experiment, Asriel woke as a Golden Flower without a SOUL, to then be called Flowey. I will not talk about the 1st Royal Scientist, as most of thier story is of fan creation and speculation.

The Present

In Undertale, there are three routes. A Pacifist route, a Neutral route, and finally a Genocide route. I will explain the criteria needed for each run, and then the story of each. I just want to make something clear: There is no "Canon" timeline. In game, there are times characters seem to know that they are in a game. Sans, Flowey, Genocide Chara, they all are aware of the player being able to RESET and SAVE. Sans sole purpose in his boss fight is to make you so bored or frustrated that you quit. Before Flowey's Neutral route boss fight, the game crashes, with there being effects on the game browser and the game, showing how much Flowey had at that point. Chara will effect your game after a Genocide route, if you complete a Pacifist route game, which is called the SOULless Pacifist route. Now, here are the three routes.

All routes start the same. The Player Character (PC) wakes up in a bed of golden flowers. They walk to a door, and then meet Flowey the Flower. Flower proceeds to lie to the PC, while also being the tutorial of the game. Toriel saves the PC, and leads them to the ruins. She shows them a puzzle whilst solving it, then leads the PC forward. She leads us across a bridge with a trap, then in a hallway is where the routes diverge, into two groups.

The Pacifist Route

The Pacifist route requires the Player not to harm any monster. This means that the Player has to rely on MERCY, signified by a yellow name tag after a number of ACTS. The first fight is with a Froggit. The PC finishes the fight with MERCY, then continues down the hallway. Then Toriel sends the PC down a hallway, after she runs ahead. PC reaches the end of the hallway, and Toriel reveals herself from behind a pillar in the room. Then she informs the PC that she will leave them alone to attend to some buisness, but not before handing the PC a phone. The PC then completes multiple puzzles, and SPAREs many monsters. During this process, Toriel calls the PC to ask if they like Butterscotch or Cinnamon more and if they have any allergies. Then they reach Home, the old Home of the Royal Family and Toriel's current home. Toriel takes the PC inside and then to a room that Toriel says is the PC's. Then the PC sleeps, wakes up and sees a slice of pie on the floor. The PC then heads over to Toriel in the Family Room and asks several times how to return to the Surface. Toriel excuses herself to head downstairs, in which the PC follows. Then, after following Toriel, they reach a door. Toriel then fights the PC. If the PC gets to low HP, then Toriel starts to intentionally whiff her attacks. When we SPARE her, she gives us a hug then we leave the Ruins.

After leaving the Ruins, the PC enters Snowdin Forest. They soon discover that two Royal Guards, Sans and Papyrus, are looking for the human. Sans is the older brother of Papyrus, who wants to become a member of the Royal Guard. Papyrus is determined to capture the PC, while Sans is much more relaxed about the situation. Throughout the forest, Papyrus sets up several puzzles and traps in an attempt to capture the PC. However, the PC is able to get past them, and Sans often helps them without Papyrus noticing. Eventually, Papyrus confronts the PC and begins his battle. Despite his attempts to capture them, Papyrus does not actually want to seriously hurt the PC. The PC can eventually SPARE him, after which Papyrus decides that the two of them should become friends. He takes the PC to his house in Snowdin, where they spend some time together. Papyrus then allows the PC to continue their journey.The PC explores the town of Snowdin and eventually reaches the bridge leading out of the area. There, Papyrus challenges them again, this time as a member of the Royal Guard. After the PC continues to refuse to fight him, Papyrus eventually gives up and lets them pass. The PC then continues onward toward Waterfall, leaving Snowdin behind.

After leaving Snowdin, the PC enters Waterfall. The area is filled with glowing plants, rivers, waterfalls, and ancient writings about the history of humans and monsters. As they travel through Waterfall, they encounter Undyne, the captain of the Royal Guard, who is determined to capture the human and bring them to King Asgore. The PC attempts to avoid Undyne as they travel through the area, while also encountering several monsters along the way. Eventually, Undyne catches up to them and begins pursuing them through Waterfall. The PC reaches a bridge, where Undyne attacks them. After the PC falls into the water below, they eventually continue onward. The PC later encounters Monster Kid, a young monster who looks up to Undyne. Monster Kid follows the PC for part of their journey and talks about Undyne, unaware of the full danger the PC is in. Eventually, Undyne catches up with them again. When Monster Kid falls from a ledge, the PC can choose to save them. Undyne then prepares to attack the PC, but Monster Kid stands between them. Undyne pushes Monster Kid aside and continues the fight.After the PC escapes, Undyne follows them into Hotland. However, the heat causes Undyne to collapse. The PC can give her water, allowing her to recover. If the PC does so, Undyne later appears at Papyrus's house, where she can be befriended after Papyrus introduces the two of them. The PC then continues their journey toward Hotland.

After leaving Waterfall, the PC enters Hotland, a large area filled with lava, machinery, and various puzzles. Soon after arriving, they receive a phone call from Alphys, the Royal Scientist. Alphys begins communicating with the PC and attempts to help them through Hotland, although she is often nervous and awkward while doing so. As the PC travels through Hotland, they encounter Mettaton, a television robot created by Alphys. Mettaton repeatedly appears to challenge the PC, turning their encounters into television shows. During these encounters, the PC must complete various challenges while Alphys continues to communicate with them and provide assistance. Eventually, the PC reaches Alphys's laboratory. Inside, they discover information about Alphys's experiments, including the creation of the Amalgamates. Alphys explains that she had attempted to use Determination in experiments involving monsters that had been severely injured, hoping to prevent them from dying. However, the experiments caused the monsters to melt together, creating the Amalgamates. Alphys had hidden the results of the experiments from the other monsters because she was ashamed of what had happened.The PC eventually encounters the Amalgamates while exploring the laboratory and learns that they are actually monsters whose families believe them to be dead. After continuing through the laboratory, the PC leaves and continues deeper into Hotland.

After leaving the laboratory, the PC continues through Hotland and eventually reaches the CORE, a massive power facility that provides energy for the Underground. The CORE contains numerous puzzles and dangerous machinery, while Mettaton continues to interfere with the PC's journey through his television programs. As the PC makes their way through the CORE, they receive calls from Alphys, who continues attempting to guide them. Mettaton eventually appears again and reveals that his goal is to become famous by defeating the human. His encounters with the PC become increasingly elaborate as he attempts to turn their journey into entertainment. The PC eventually reaches the end of the CORE and enters the MTT Resort, a large hotel and entertainment area operated by Mettaton. The PC encounters Sans at the resort, who invites them to eat at a restaurant. Afterward, the PC continues through the resort and eventually reaches the entrance to the Citadel. Before the PC can continue, Mettaton appears in his Mettaton EX form and begins his final battle against them. Alphys secretly helps the PC by revealing that Mettaton's body has a switch that can turn him into his EX form. During the battle, the PC can impress the audience by completing various actions rather than harming Mettaton. Eventually, Mettaton's ratings become high enough for him to stop the battle. After realizing that his new body is consuming too much energy, Mettaton leaves the PC and allows them to continue toward the Citadel.

After defeating Mettaton EX, the PC continues through the MTT Resort. The PC encounters Sans again, who invites them to eat at Grillby's. Afterward, the PC continues through the resort and eventually reaches the entrance to the Citadel. However, because the PC is completing the Pacifist Route, there are still events they must complete before continuing to the end of the game. The PC can return to Snowdin and find Papyrus, who explains that Undyne wants to deliver a letter to Alphys. The PC takes the letter to Alphys's laboratory, where Alphys becomes nervous after discovering who sent it. The PC then helps Alphys with her feelings for Undyne, eventually leading to the two of them becoming friends. Afterward, Alphys receives a call from Undyne and leaves the laboratory. The PC follows her and discovers that Alphys has disappeared into the bathroom. When the PC searches for her, they discover a hidden elevator leading to the True Laboratory. Inside the True Laboratory, the PC discovers the truth behind Alphys's experiments. After Asriel became Flowey, Alphys was given the six human SOULs collected by Asgore and was ordered to find a way to use their Determination. She extracted Determination from the SOULs and injected it into monsters who had fallen down. She also injected Determination into a golden flower taken from the King's garden. The experiments initially appeared successful, but the monsters began to melt together because of the Determination inside them. These monsters became the Amalgamates. Alphys hid them inside the True Laboratory and lied to their families about what had happened to them. As the PC explores the laboratory, they encounter the Amalgamates and learns what happened to them. They eventually discover the recordings documenting Alphys's experiments and the creation of Flowey. The PC then finds the elevator and escapes the True Laboratory. After leaving the laboratory, the PC returns to the castle to continue toward the Barrier. With the necessary friendships completed, the PC is now able to reach the true ending of their journey. The PC returns to the Citadel and travels through New Home toward Asgore's castle. Along the way, they encounter several monsters who tell stories about the Royal Family and the history of the Underground. Eventually, the PC reaches the throne room and meets Asgore. Asgore prepares to fight the PC in order to take their SOUL and break the Barrier. Before the battle can properly begin, Toriel arrives and interrupts him. Sans, Papyrus, Undyne, and Alphys soon arrive as well. The group is interrupted when Flowey appears and attacks them. Flowey absorbs the six human SOULs and the SOULs of the monsters nearby, transforming into Asriel Dreemurr. Asriel then transforms into his most powerful form, the God of Hyperdeath. The PC is unable to defeat Asriel through normal combat, so they use the SAVE ability to reach their friends and restore their memories. After saving their friends, the PC reaches Asriel himself. Asriel remembers his past as the prince of the Underground and realizes that the PC is not Chara. The PC continues refusing to give up on him, eventually causing Asriel to stop fighting. Asriel releases the SOULs and uses his power to destroy the Barrier. Monsterkind is finally freed from the Underground. Asriel returns to his normal form and speaks with the PC before eventually returning to the castle. The PC wakes up on the Surface after the Barrier has been destroyed. Toriel, Sans, Papyrus, Undyne, Alphys, and the other monsters eventually emerge from the Underground. Monsterkind is now free and can begin a new life on the Surface.

The Genocide Route

The Genocide Route requires the Player to deliberately kill every monster they encounter in each area. This means that the PC must continue fighting until the area is exhausted of encounters. The game gives several signs that the route is being completed, including changes to dialogue, music, and the number of monsters remaining. Unlike the Pacifist Route, the PC does not use MERCY to resolve encounters. The route also changes several major boss fights and causes many characters to react differently to the PC.

All routes begin in the same way, but the Genocide Route begins to separate from the others in the Ruins. Instead of following Toriel's lessons about MERCY, the PC begins deliberately searching for monsters to fight. They continue encountering monsters until the Ruins are exhausted. Eventually, the game displays the message "But nobody came." The PC then encounters Toriel. Because the PC has killed every monster in the Ruins, Toriel is killed in a single attack.

After leaving the Ruins, the PC enters Snowdin Forest. The area is noticeably different from a normal playthrough. The music changes, the puzzles and dialogue are altered, and the monsters begin reacting differently to the PC. Sans eventually realizes that something is wrong and becomes much more serious than he was during the other routes. The PC continues killing monsters until the Snowdin area is exhausted. Papyrus then confronts the PC. Unlike the other routes, Papyrus does not fight them. Instead, he believes that the PC can still change and offers to forgive them. The PC kills Papyrus.

After leaving Snowdin, the PC enters Waterfall. The area continues to change as the PC eliminates its monsters. Monster Kid encounters the PC and continues to look up to Undyne. When Monster Kid stands between the PC and Undyne, Undyne pushes Monster Kid out of the way. She then realizes that she must stop the PC herself. The PC attacks Undyne, but she refuses to die and transforms into Undyne the Undying. This form is created through her determination to protect Monsterkind. The PC eventually defeats and kills Undyne the Undying, allowing them to continue through Waterfall.

After leaving Waterfall, the PC enters Hotland. The area is largely empty because the monsters have begun fleeing from the PC. Alphys has been warned about the PC and attempts to evacuate the monsters of Hotland and the CORE. Mettaton confronts the PC in his Mettaton NEO form, intending to fight them and protect the remaining monsters. However, the PC defeats Mettaton NEO in a single attack before he can properly fight back.

The PC continues through the CORE, killing the remaining monsters and Royal Guards along the way. Eventually, they reach the MTT Resort and continue toward the Citadel. The route has become increasingly different from the normal game, with many characters either dead or missing. The PC eventually reaches the entrance to New Home, where Sans is waiting.

Before reaching the final battle, the PC travels through New Home and encounters monsters who describe the history of the Underground. Eventually, they reach Sans, who reveals that he knows about the PC's actions and their ability to RESET. Sans then begins his boss fight, attempting to prevent the PC from reaching Asgore and destroying the world. Unlike most battles, Sans constantly avoids the PC's attacks and uses unusual attacks that take advantage of the game's mechanics. After a long battle, the PC eventually kills Sans.

The PC continues to the throne room, where they find Asgore. Asgore is unaware of what has happened and prepares to begin the fight. Before he can do anything, Flowey kills Asgore and takes his SOUL. Flowey then realizes that the PC is not Chara and begs for mercy. The PC kills Flowey as well.

After Flowey dies, the PC encounters Chara. Chara explains that the PC's actions awakened them and that the power gained from killing monsters has allowed them to return. Chara then destroys the world, ending the Genocide Route. After this point, the game can be RESET, but completing another playthrough can be affected by the events of the Genocide Route.
