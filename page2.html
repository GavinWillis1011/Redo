What are Lietmotifs?

Leitmotifs are a reacurring musical "phrase" that help listeners identify when a character or place is related to another. They were popularised by Richard Wagner, who used them to psycologically deepen his music. In the game Undertale, Toby Fox uses leitmotifs to deepen conections between characters and places. His music has multiple connections that effect the story of Undertale in the player's view. Examples of this are:

  • There are areas of the boss fight music of both Undyne and Toriel, in Asgore's boss music, but none of Asgores song in the either of the two. This shows how Asgore has kept himself away from the world, and while he cares about Monsterkind, Monsterkind only knows him as the King.
  • In the song Hopes and Dreams, the Phase 1 song of Asriel Dremurr, there is a obvious connection to Once Upon A Time, a song that's leitmotif is reused in many songs, and also a connection to Snowy, the song for the Town Snowdinn. These connections are to point out how this boss fight came to be, with all the Underground's residints, except for you , being absorbed into Asriel. Snowdinn is the only full town fuly scene in-game.
  • When you meet the character Flowey at the beginning of the game, the song Your Best Freind plays, with a very obvious leitmotif. In the Neutral Ending, Flowey is a boss, the boss song called Your Worst Nightmare, with the same leitmotif. This is an example of Toby Fox using leitmotifs to connect a characters intentions to moments, as from the start of the game the character's intentions were obvious yet not intimidating. In the boss song, the intentions are both.
  • About Flowey and Asriel, the fact they are one and the same is also foreshadowed through the music, even including how the lore sees it. In Finale and SAVE the World (Phase 1 and Phase 2 of the Asriel Dreamurr fight respectivally), Floweys leitmotif is there, while Asriels leitmotif, in the song His Theme, is not in any of Flowey's songs. This is lore accurate, as in the story, Asriel lost his SOUL, and became Flowey. Flowey, in order to become the boss Asreil Dreamurr, had to absorb almost all of the Underground's SOULs, with the sole exception of the player's character. So in the fight, Asriel is not who he was, showing growth. Only after the fight ends with the boss being SPARED, then His Theme plays while talking to the quelled Asriel. He has a soul, albiet not his own, but now has emotions.
